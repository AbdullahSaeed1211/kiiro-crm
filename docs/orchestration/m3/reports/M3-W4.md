# M3-W4 report

The leaf intake, mail, and scheduled-job behavior is implemented and locally verified. Intake covers bounded payloads, exact origins, server keys, Turnstile, hashed rate limiting, same-day dedupe, lead creation, message comments, and notifications. Mail covers tenant-scoped record addresses, MIME parsing, duplicate suppression, sender checks, quarantine and release, escaped templates, console-compatible sending, Resend fallback, and platform-domain routing. The Cloudflare adapter also exposes six job constructors and a complete registry.

The branch deliberately stops at the integration boundary. Central Payload registration, generated files, migrations, the Worker entry, the application mail-router entry, route composition, settings UI, and seed assembly are lead-owned by the M3 plan and critical-path rules.
