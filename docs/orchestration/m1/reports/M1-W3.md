# Report: M1-W3

The adapter now has everything the brief lists, and all of it is unit tested (59 tests):

- **Senders:** `CloudflareMailSender` (spec §14.2 error mapping), `ConsoleMailSender` and a platform-sender fallback wrapper.
- **Inbound email:** an inbound sink port with a logging sink.
- **Internal requests:** a constant-time internal secret check, plus request handlers for both internal routes.
- **Cron:** a dispatcher that isolates failures per job, and the `tasks.dueSoon` job. The job writes through `NotificationStore` with a dedupe key built from the record, the due date in the tenant time zone and the user.

Both Next routes build and are listed. Every gate passes except knip, which reports only pre-existing findings.

Look at these first:

1. **Both routes return 503 until wired.** They delegate through `apps/web/src/app/api/v1/internal/handlers.ts`, which re-exports fail-closed stand-ins from `unwired.ts`. After the barrel exports in the front matter are added, change `'./unwired'` to `'@ops/adapter-cloudflare'` in `handlers.ts` and delete `unwired.ts`.
2. **The due-soon job has no cursor past 200 items per run** (open question 3).
3. **Most Email Service error codes map to `UNAVAILABLE`.** That follows the brief, but several of those codes are not transient (open question 2).
